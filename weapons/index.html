Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the potential penalties for a firearm conviction in Virginia Beach, VA?

A conviction can lead to significant prison time, heavy fines, and the permanent loss of your right to possess a firearm. The severity depends on the specific charge, such as whether it is a misdemeanor for a first-time concealed carry violation or a felony for possession by a convicted felon. Fellers Law works to mitigate these consequences by building a robust defense strategy tailored to your specific case.

Can a weapon and firearm defense attorney help if my gun was found during a vehicle search?

Yes. If the police conducted a search of your vehicle without probable cause or a valid warrant, any evidence they found—including a weapon—may be suppressed and deemed inadmissible in court. We carefully review the details of your traffic stop and search to ensure your Fourth Amendment rights were protected and seek to have charges dismissed if law enforcement overstepped their authority.

Is it possible to have my firearm rights restored after a felony conviction?

In many cases, it is possible to petition the court for a restoration of your civil rights and firearm privileges. The process is complex and requires demonstrating a period of good behavior and a compelling reason for restoration. As your weapon and firearm defense lawyer, we can guide you through the legal requirements and represent you during the petition process to help you regain your Second Amendment rights.

What is considered a concealed weapon under Virginia law?

Virginia law defines a concealed weapon as any firearm or dangerous instrument hidden from common observation on your person or within your immediate reach, such as in a glove compartment or under a seat. However, there are specific exceptions for individuals with valid permits or those transporting weapons in certain ways. If you are facing charges, Fellers Law can help determine if an exception applies to your situation.
