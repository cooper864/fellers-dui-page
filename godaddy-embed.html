Your Next Steps

What to Do After a DUI Arrest

After an arrest, it's easy to feel overwhelmed. Taking a few practical steps now can help protect your rights later.

  1. Keep all paperwork you receive from law enforcement and the court.
  2. Write down what happened before, during, and after the traffic stop while it's still fresh.
  3. Avoid discussing your case with friends or on social media.
  4. Contact an experienced DUI lawyer before making statements that could be used against you.

The sooner your attorney begins reviewing your case, the sooner important evidence can be preserved. Traffic stop details, body camera footage, testing procedures, and witness accounts may all play a role in your defense.
